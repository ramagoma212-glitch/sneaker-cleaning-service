Cleaning Tips

5 Ways to Keep Your Sneakers Clean

Remove loose dirt regularly, deal with marks early, use a soft brush, avoid harsh chemicals and always check the care instructions for the material.

White Sneakers

How to Care for White Sneakers

Clean white sneakers gently, use suitable products and allow them to air-dry away from direct heat. Test new products on a hidden area first.

Smart Habits

Make Your Favourite Sneakers Last Longer

Rotate pairs when possible, store sneakers in a dry place and clean dirt before it becomes heavily embedded in the material.

Quick tip: Avoid putting every sneaker in a washing machine. Some materials, glues and shapes can be damaged by aggressive washing or heat.
